# Warranty

## What is covered
- Frame: lifetime warranty for the original owner against manufacturing defects.
- Motor, battery, controller and display: 2 years from the delivery date.
- All other components (brakes, drivetrain, wheels, lights): 1 year.

The battery warranty covers failure and capacity loss below 70% of the original capacity within the 2 years.

## What is not covered
Normal wear parts (tires, tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes, grips), damage from crashes, water damage from pressure washing or submerging the bike, and any damage from using a non-Larkspur battery or charger. Commercial use such as delivery work voids the warranty on the motor and battery.

## Making a claim
Email warranty@larkspur.example with your order number, a description of the problem and a short video if possible. Most claims get an answer within 3 business days. Approved parts ship free.

## Transferring the warranty
The 2-year and 1-year warranties transfer to a second owner if the bike is sold within those periods. The lifetime frame warranty does not transfer.
