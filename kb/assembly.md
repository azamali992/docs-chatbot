# Assembly

Bikes arrive about 85% assembled. Most people finish assembly in 30 to 45 minutes.

## In the box
Bike, front wheel, handlebar, pedals, front fender, headlight, battery, charger, a multi-tool and a 15mm wrench for the pedals.

## Steps
1. Attach the handlebar to the stem with the four stem bolts. Torque to 5 Nm.
2. Fit the front wheel and tighten the thru-axle.
3. Install the pedals. The left pedal is reverse-threaded (turn it counter-clockwise to tighten).
4. Attach the front fender and headlight.
5. Charge the battery fully before the first ride.

A full video walkthrough is at larkspur.example/assembly.
