# Battery and charging

## Battery specs
All current models use a 48V 14Ah (672Wh) removable battery. The Larkspur Tempo and Larkspur Ridge use the same battery, so spares are interchangeable. The older Larkspur Classic (sold before 2024) uses a 36V battery that is not compatible.

## Range
Expect 35–60 miles (55–95 km) per charge. Range depends on rider weight, hills, wind, tire pressure and assist level. Riding mostly in assist level 1 gives the top of that range. Throttle-only riding gives about 20–25 miles.

## Charging
A full charge from empty takes about 5 to 6 hours with the included 3A charger. The optional 5A fast charger (sold separately, $119) cuts that to about 3.5 hours.

## Storage and care
- Store the battery between 40% and 70% charge if you won't ride for more than 2 weeks.
- Keep it indoors between 50°F and 77°F (10°C–25°C). Do not charge below 32°F (0°C).
- Top it up every 2 months during long storage.
- A battery typically keeps about 80% of its capacity after 800 full charge cycles.

## Flying and shipping a battery
Our batteries are over 100Wh and cannot be taken on commercial flights, checked or carry-on.
