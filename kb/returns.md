# Returns and refunds

## 30-day return window
You can return any e-bike within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as it has fewer than 50 miles (80 km) on the odometer and has no damage beyond normal test riding.

## How to start a return
Email returns@larkspur.example with your order number. We send a prepaid return label and a box within 2 business days. Pack the bike in the original packaging if you still have it.

## Return shipping cost
Return shipping is free in the US lower 48. For Alaska, Hawaii, Canada and the UK, a $99 (or £79) return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the bike reaches our warehouse and passes inspection. Financing refunds are handled through Affirm and can take up to 10 business days.

## Accessories
Unused accessories can be returned within 60 days. Helmets cannot be returned once worn, for safety reasons.
