# Shipping and delivery

## Where we ship
Larkspur Cycles ships to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. We do not currently ship to the EU, Australia or anywhere else.

## Delivery times
- US (lower 48 states): 3–6 business days.
- Alaska and Hawaii: 7–12 business days.
- Canada: 5–9 business days.
- United Kingdom: 4–8 business days.

Bikes leave our Portland, Oregon warehouse within 2 business days of the order.

## Shipping cost
Shipping is free on every e-bike to the US lower 48. Alaska and Hawaii cost $149. Canada costs CA$99. UK shipping is £79 and import duty is included in the price, so there is nothing extra to pay on delivery.

Accessories ship free on orders over $75. Below that, accessory shipping is a flat $8.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by email as soon as the bike leaves the warehouse. Bikes ship with FedEx Freight in the US and with DPD in the UK. A signature is required on delivery.
