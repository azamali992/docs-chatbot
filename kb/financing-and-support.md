# Financing, payments and support

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Shop Pay.

## Financing
US customers can pay over time with Affirm: 6 or 12 months at 0% APR for qualified buyers, or up to 36 months with interest. Financing is not available in Canada or the UK.

## Price matching
We do not price match other retailers. If we lower the price of your bike within 14 days of your order, email us and we refund the difference.

## Contacting support
- Email: support@larkspur.example (replies within 1 business day)
- Phone: +1 (503) 555-0142, Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm Pacific
- Live chat on the website, same hours

## Service and repairs
Larkspur does not run its own repair shops. Any bike shop can service our bikes, since they use standard parts apart from the motor and battery. For motor or battery problems, contact support first so it is handled under warranty.
