# Models and sizing

## Larkspur Tempo (commuter)
- Price: $1,599
- Motor: 500W rear hub, 65 Nm torque
- Top assisted speed: 20 mph (Class 2), can be set to 28 mph (Class 3)
- Weight: 58 lb (26 kg) with battery
- Max rider + cargo load: 300 lb (136 kg)
- Sizes: Medium (rider height 5'2"–5'9") and Large (5'9"–6'4")

## Larkspur Ridge (all-terrain)
- Price: $1,899
- Motor: 750W rear hub, 85 Nm torque
- Top assisted speed: 20 mph (Class 2)
- 4-inch fat tires, front suspension fork with 80mm travel
- Weight: 68 lb (31 kg) with battery
- Max rider + cargo load: 330 lb (150 kg)
- One size, fits riders 5'4"–6'5"

## Which one should I get?
Tempo is lighter and better for paved commutes. Ridge is for gravel, sand, snow and trails, and handles steeper hills because of the extra torque.

Both bikes come in Slate Grey and Moss Green.
